# 🔄 SECTION 2: THE SHOPIFY-STYLE PUBLISHING LIFECYCLE

In **Publishing OS 4.0**, Publish is the cockpit and Growth OS is the engine. The writer writes; the system connects the strategic dots automatically.

```text
📝 Click Articles ➔ ➕ New Article ➔ ✍️ Write Content ➔ 🟢 Publish
```

### The Recommended Workflow:
1. **Open Articles**: Click `📰 Articles` $\rightarrow$ `+`.
2. **Write Content**: Fill in Title, Content body, Excerpt, upload Featured Image with Alt text, and select Category & Author (auto-defaults to Arslan Rehmani).
3. **Primary Keyword (Optional)**: Open the collapsed `🧠 Growth OS & Intelligence` panel at the bottom to enter your target term. The system displays a yellow warning banner automatically if another post targets that keyword.
4. **Publish**: Click the green **`Publish`** button!

# ⚡ SECTION 3: STANDARD PUBLISHING WORKFLOW (The Essentials)

When writing an Article or Playbook, **95% of your work happens in the primary publishing fields**.

### Step 1: Main Content
* **Fields**: `Title`, `Content`, `Excerpt`
* **What to do**: Type your main headline, write or paste your rich text content, and provide a 1–2 sentence summary.

### Step 2: Search Engine Listing Card
* **Fields**: `Page title`, `Meta description`, `URL handle`, `Canonical URL Override`
* **Behavior**: `URL handle` auto-syncs from Title. `Page title` and `Meta description` fall back to Title & Excerpt automatically if left blank.

### Step 3: Organization & Media Card
* **Fields**: `Featured Image`, `Category`, `Author`, `Tags`, `Published Date`
* **Behavior**: Author automatically defaults to **Arslan Rehmani**. Select Category and add Tags.

### Step 4: Click Publish!
Click the green **`Publish`** button at the bottom right corner!

# ⚙️ SECTION 5: AUTOMATED INFRASTRUCTURE (What Happens Automatically)

* ⏱️ **Estimated Reading Time**: Calculated automatically on page render (`Math.ceil(words / 200)` min read).
* 👤 **Author Defaulting**: Auto-assigns *Arslan Rehmani* reference.
* 📅 **Updated Timestamp**: Auto-synced with Sanity `_updatedAt`.
* 🗺️ **XML Sitemap**: Auto-updated with every publish event.
* 🔍 **Meta Tags & OpenGraph Cards**: Auto-generated from Title, Excerpt, and Cover Image.
* 📜 **Structured Data (Schema.org)**: Auto-generates valid JSON-LD tags (`Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Organization`).
